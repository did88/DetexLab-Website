CAMPAIGN PURPOSE

Connect awareness
to an on-site response.

The campaign is not a product-sales page or a guarantee of safety. Its purpose is to help people recognize risk, seek help when something feels wrong, and connect to the institution's response process.

  • Explain drink spiking and possible warning signs
  • Show participants and staff an actionable response path
  • When an approved trial exists, describe the prototype's screening role accurately

PROGRAM MODULES

Four campaign modules,
combined for the setting

Select only the modules within the organization's approved scope, then adapt the wording to the actual operating team and process.

01 · AWARENESS

Risk awareness

Explain drink spiking, situations to watch, and possible symptoms in concise, accessible content.

02 · RESPONSE

Path to assistance

Connect to the actual on-site staff, medical support, and reporting channels for the setting.

03 · PILOT

Prototype trial

Only for an approved trial: explain the agreed procedure, scope, and limits of the screening aid together.

04 · PARTNERSHIP

Institutional collaboration

Connect the organization to a process for defining education, operations, design, and trial scope together.

IF SOMETHING FEELS WRONG

Put safety before
any screening step.

On-site guidance must be reviewed against local requirements and the organization's approved safety process.

  1. 01

    Stop drinking

    If you feel unusual or suspect the drink, do not consume any more.

  2. 02

    Do not stay alone

    Remain with someone you trust and notify venue staff immediately.

  3. 03

    Ask for appropriate help

    For serious symptoms or danger, seek help from local emergency medical or law-enforcement services.

  4. 04

    Prioritize confirmation

    Do not rely on a prototype color signal alone; follow medical or forensic testing when needed.
