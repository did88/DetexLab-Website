CAPABILITY DIRECTION

One signal system,
three capability directions

These are not specifications for a finished product on sale. They describe directions being evaluated or developed for the field-trial prototype.

01

Visible color screening

Designed to translate an interaction with certain substances into a color signal that can support an initial field check.

Core capability in development
02

Portable paper format

A paper-based format is being developed for potential use in settings such as events, campuses, and institutional programs.

Prototype direction
03

Organization-specific format

Form factor, visual design, and logo application may be proposed through collaboration based on an organization's use context.

CONCEPTUAL WORKFLOW

How it is intended to fit
into a field response

Exact sample amount, contact, reaction and reading times, reuse, and disposal instructions are not finalized. Approved trials follow a separately agreed protocol.

  1. 01

    Notice the context

    Observe the drink and any unusual symptoms, and stop consumption if something seems wrong.

  2. 02

    Follow an agreed protocol

    Use a prototype only under the instructions approved for the institutional trial.

  3. 03

    Read a screening signal

    Interpret a color change only as a field-screening signal of possible presence.

  4. 04

    Connect to safety response

    Prioritize the institution's safety process and confirmatory medical or forensic testing when needed.

USE WITH CONTEXT

What to know
before any feature

A screening aid

A Detex color signal does not replace a legal, medical, or forensic determination.

Not a safety guarantee

No color change does not establish that a drink or situation is safe.

Specifications pending

Reading criteria and detailed instructions will be provided after validation and product specifications are finalized.
