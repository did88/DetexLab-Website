WHY DETEX

Invisible risks need
an immediate on-site check

Drink spiking means adding drugs or alcohol to someone’s drink without their knowledge or consent. Before confirmatory laboratory analysis, simple tools are needed for rapid field screening.

HOW IT WORKS

How it works

When certain drugs in a beverage interact with the sensor, the detection strip changes color. This website provides only a general public-facing explanation; chemical structures, synthesis methods, formulation ratios, and manufacturing conditions are not disclosed.

Detailed instructions will be released once product specifications are finalized.

Before use Pale blue
Upon reaction Shifts toward pink

This is a conceptual color-change illustration, not a confirmed interpretation standard for individual drugs.

DEVELOPMENT STATUS

From four demonstrated targets
toward six

Detection results have been demonstrated for four target substances: GHB, ketamine, MDMA, and methamphetamine. Development is underway to expand the platform to six targets, including alkyl nitrites and Rohypnol.

ESSENTIAL SAFETY NOTICE

No color change does not guarantee that a drink is safe. This product is not a substitute for legal, forensic, or medical confirmation.

Instructions

Detailed instructions will be released once product specifications are finalized.

Items not yet finalized

Exact beverage volume, number of drops, contact time, reaction time, final read time, reusability, and disposal method

Color-change guidance

A color change is a screening signal indicating the possible presence of a target substance. It must not be used as a confirmed interpretation standard for individual drugs.
